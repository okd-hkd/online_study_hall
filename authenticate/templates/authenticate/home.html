What is Online Study Hall?

Online Study Hall is a weekly online event for English learners. In order to help you learn English, a tutor is answers questions you may have.

This study event aims to motivate the learners by regulary studying together with other learners and ask questions to a tutor regarding English language.

Please register as a user to see details. Then download Zoom to access Online Study Hall! (The URL will be sent before the study session starts.)
