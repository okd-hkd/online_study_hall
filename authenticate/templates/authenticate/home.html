What is Online Study Hall?

Online Study Hall is a weekly online event for English learners. In order to help you learn English, a tutor answers to the questions you may have.

This study event aims to motivate the learners by regulary studying together with other learners. Learners can ask their questions to the tutor regarding English language.

Please register as a user if you are interested in. We will invite you to Online Study Hall.
